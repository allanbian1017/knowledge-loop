# Glossary

Operational locations are resolved through external configuration. Content-workflow traces, captures and review artifacts belong under external ScratchRoot; Home Repo scratch is reserved for nonpersonal development. The Dreamer and memory-injection concepts below are architectural proposals, not background services installed by this migration.

## Trace
A structured `.json` or `.md` artifact generated by complex skills (e.g., `agent-browser`) at the end of their execution. It logs task intent, intermediate tool calls, and final success/failure state for authorized diagnostics. Personal traces stay outside the public checkout, for example under `ScratchRoot/traces/`.

## Dreamer
An asynchronous batch-processing script (or skill) that runs during downtime (e.g., as part of the `daily-workflow`). It consumes accumulated `Trace` files, evaluates them using an LLM to identify recurring errors or anti-patterns, and extracts permanent rules to prevent future failures.

## Memory Injection
The process of permanently storing the rules extracted by the `Dreamer`. To prevent global context bloat, the rule is appended locally to the specific skill's `SKILL.md` (e.g., under a `## Known Gotchas & Lessons` section). Concurrently, the skill's `README.md` changelog must be updated to reflect the newly injected rule.
